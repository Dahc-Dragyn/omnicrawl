## 1. Market Fundamentals & The Vulnerability

* **The Economics:** The housing stock in Fishers Landing and Cascade Park consists heavily of homes built in the late 1990s. At 25 to 30 years old, these properties are passing the expiration date for original builder-grade kitchens and bathrooms. The demographic possesses high home equity and median incomes capable of supporting $40,000 to $80,000 renovations.
* **The Vulnerability (Trust):** Homeowners face extreme anxiety when deploying massive capital for remodeling. The fear of "fly-by-night" contractors, abandoned projects, and uninsured damages creates massive friction.
* **The Moat:** Current aggregators (Google Maps, Yelp) do not verify state licensure or bond amounts. They rely solely on easily manipulated star ratings.
